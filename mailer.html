BarberCo Mailer

BarberCo Mailer is the email delivery integration for The Barber Co appointment website. It sends account verification codes, password-reset codes, and appointment notifications from the shop's authorized Gmail sender.

Only the sender account's operator authorizes Google access. Customers do not grant access to their Google accounts. The integration requests Gmail's send-only permission; it does not request permission to read or search the sender's inbox.

Customer accounts and booking records are managed by the barbershop website, not by Google sign-in.
